Performance Metrics Explanation

Accuracy

Measures the proportion of total predictions that are correct.

Precision

Measures how many predicted approvals were actually approved in the test dataset.

Recall

Measures how many actual approvals were correctly identified.

F1-Score

Combines precision and recall into a single performance measure.
